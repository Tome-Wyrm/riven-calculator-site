## Reading the stat panel

- **Arrows:** a row reading `139% > 262%` is previous > new, after a config or mod change. The right-hand number is what the mods on screen give. It may still be a mid-video edit rather than a saved build, so check the tab and whether the number stays.
- **Precision Intensify:** adds 90 Strength to the ability in the 4th slot only, whichever ability that is. A Helminth ability can be subsumed into any slot, so it gets the 90 there. The panel leaves it out: it shows the general Strength total, and this bonus appears only in the ability's hover. In the Sevagoth video he says 145 for everything except Roar, which is at 235, and Roar then reaches 70% (0.3 x 2.35). On Excalibur he says it brings Exalted Blade to effectively 275, which is the panel's 185 + 90. Follie's 139 and 223 and Lavos's 170 add up from their mods without it. Hildryn's 100 and Sirius & Orion's 184 add up once their four Strength shards are counted, and Revenant's 284 with two. Hildryn's video notes two snapshotting quirks: Aegis Storm casting her 1 passes the bonus to it, and Arcane Expertise counts the 90 if the last cast was the 4. Excalibur's 185 is suspect: it's 90 more than its mods give (100 - 60 + 55) with no Strength shards, possibly an Arsenal or Simulacrum bug that no longer exists. Sevagoth's numbers from Discord, 2026-10-26, with Roar, for a 400% multiplier: Strength 254.02% without Precision Intensify, 216.67% with it (`(1 + 0.5S)(1 + 0.3(S + 0.9)) = 4`, the 0.3 factor being subsumed Roar in the 4th slot). S there is Strength without the 90.

- **What the arrow compares:** the left number is the build the Arsenal was loaded with, the right number the tab being viewed. Backing out and going back in equips the viewed build, which then becomes the left number. Limbo with Succ equipped reads `183 > 178` on Sunder and `183 > 40` on Boxer. Drops are red and rises green.
- **Conditionals:** almost none show on the panel, only those met by base stats. The Arsenal doesn't even count as max energy. So a panel value is a floor, and conditional Strength has to come from the build's mods and arcanes.
- **Snapshotting:** an ability keeps the Strength it was cast with until it expires, and later changes don't reach it (wiki: Snapshotting). So a temporary Strength bonus counts for as long as the ability cast under it lasts.
- **Molt Augmented:** not on the panel at full value. Each kill adds Strength, up to +60 at 250 kills, so Mag's 84 becomes 144.
